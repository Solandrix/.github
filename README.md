## Solandrix

Solandrix is a Bucharest-based software consultancy founded in 2025 by Dragoș and Lorelai Niculai. We build custom software, systems integrations and AI automation for companies in Romania and Europe.

### What we do

- [Custom Software Development](https://solandrix.com/services/custom-software/): web, mobile and internal applications built around the workflows, data and integrations that make an operation different.
- [Software Product Development](https://solandrix.com/services/product-development/): product engineering from discovery and first release through continuous development, scaling and operation.
- [AI Solutions and Automation](https://solandrix.com/services/ai-automation/): integration, rules-based automation and AI, with human review where consequences require it.
- [Systems Integration](https://solandrix.com/services/cloud-integration/): applications, platforms and data sources connected through observable, secure flows.
- [Application Modernization](https://solandrix.com/services/app-modernization/): aging applications assessed, stabilized and modernized without losing what still works.
- [Digital Transformation](https://solandrix.com/services/digital-transformation/): coordinated changes across process, systems and data, ordered around a measurable outcome.
- [IT and Business Consultancy](https://solandrix.com/services/it-consultancy/): technical audits and architecture reviews that end in a decision brief.

### How we work

- **Trade-offs stay visible.** Cost, speed, flexibility, security and maintainability are compared openly before a decision is made.
- **Delivery includes operation.** Documentation, monitoring, failure handling and post-launch support are part of the work from the start.
- **You keep ownership.** Code, documentation and project accounts belong to the client.

A typical engagement starts with a 1-2 week discovery; a first working version usually follows in 6-10 weeks.
